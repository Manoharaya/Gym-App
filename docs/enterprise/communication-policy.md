# Enterprise Communication Policies

## 1. Overview
Enterprise communication policies standardize member messaging across SMS, Email, Push Notifications, and Voice across all brands and facilities.

## 2. Guardrails & Parameters
* `quietHoursStart` & `quietHoursEnd`: Enforces mandatory quiet hours (e.g. 21:00 to 07:00) during which promotional outreach is prohibited.
* `maxPromotionsPerWeek`: Caps promotional frequency per member to reduce churn and unsubscribe rates.
* `senderIdEnforced`: Enforces verified Alphanumeric Sender IDs for SMS compliance.
* `broadcastApprovalRequired`: Multi-outlet or brand-wide mass broadcasts require explicit sign-off from a `BRAND_MANAGER` or `ENTERPRISE_ADMIN`.
