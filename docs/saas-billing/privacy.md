# Privacy & Compliance

Privacy compliance and data retention rules for SaaS commercial billing.

## Separation of Corporate and Consumer Data
- SaaS Billing records reflect B2B commercial entities (Gym Organisation legal name, billing contact, tax ID).
- Member PII and individual consumer financial records are never merged with SaaS invoices.
- Commercial invoices are subject to statutory accounting retention policies (typically 7 years) and are exempt from automatic consumer GDPR erasure requests.
