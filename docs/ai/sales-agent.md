# FitCore AI Sales Agent Foundation

## Overview
The **FitCore AI Sales Agent** is an enterprise-grade conversational sales intelligence layer designed for modern fitness businesses. It interacts with prospective members across omnichannel entry points (Web Chat, WhatsApp, SMS, Walk-ins, Receptionist Referrals), understands buyer intent and fitness goals, grounds responses in verified facility data, qualifies leads into Day 33's lead pipeline, provides transparent membership recommendations, and executes controlled next best actions (trials, tours, consultations) with human staff escalation.

The AI Sales Agent operates as a **trusted sales advisor**—not an autonomous closer. It never executes unapproved discounts, processes credit cards, modifies membership subscriptions, or diagnoses medical symptoms.

## Modality Adaptability
The AI Sales Agent dynamically adapts its persona, terminology, and recommendation heuristics across distinct fitness business models:

| Fitness Modality | Primary Focus | Key Qualification Signals | Typical Recommended Next Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traditional Health Club / 24/7 Gym** | Equipment floor, hours, flexible access | Schedule flexibility, independent training | Guided facility tour or multi-day gym pass |
| **Boutique Yoga / Pilates Studio** | Reformers, class styles, intimate coaching | Experience level, instructor style preference | Introductory class trial or starter pack |
| **CrossFit Box / Functional Fitness** | Community, coach-led WODs, progression | Athletic background, group motivation | Foundations session or trial WOD |
| **Personal Training / S&C Studio** | 1-on-1 coaching, accountability, body comp | Specific target dates, prior injury history | Complimentary assessment & trainer consult |
| **Multi-Location Fitness Brand** | Reciprocal access, passport perks | Home vs work travel frequency | Multi-club pass comparison & tour |

## Channel Support
The AI Sales Agent operates seamlessly across:
1. **Web Live Chat**: Embedded on the gym's public portal or landing page.
2. **WhatsApp**: Conversational messaging for incoming prospect inquiries.
3. **SMS**: Rapid response to mobile click-to-text campaigns.
4. **Receptionist Escalation**: Warm transfers from Day 31–35 AI Receptionist when inquiries transition from operational questions to membership buying intent.

---

## Multilingual Support
The agent provides native English and Nepali conversational capabilities, maintaining culturally appropriate formality, greeting customs (`नमस्ते`), and localized price presentation.
